\section{What's Next}
The Signals proof of concept was built during the Arbitrum Collabtech Hackathon (1st place) and continued in the Uniswap Hooks Incubator (prizes from Arbitrum and the Uniswap Foundation). Contracts and dashboard are open source.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/0xLighthouse/signals}}

We aim to formalize and validate the protocol on:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Capturing voter preference intensity and opportunity cost as sufficient risk.
  \item Locking as sybil resistance and its limits under heterogeneous token distributions.
  \item Gameable vs. productive patterns for endorsements and reputation (as an optional module).
  \item Inclusivity: how smaller voting blocks benefit from time-weighted designs and decay floors.
  \item Incentive design: robust bucket curves, interpolation strategies, and anti-gaming analysis.
  \item Bounties lifecycle: finalized refund semantics for expired bounties and contributor UX.
  \item Factory ergonomics and validation: richer config checks to prevent bricked boards.
  \item Gas and storage: empirical benchmarks for decay/claim paths and interval sizing.
\end{itemize}

Implementation work in progress:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Additional decay and incentive curve variants with formal parameter bounds.
  \item Extended board state controls and analytics surfaces (weight snapshots, per-supporter queries).
  \item Documentation alignment and examples reflecting the constructor-based pool and board approval.
\end{itemize}

If interested in collaborating on a whitepaper or formal verification, please reach out.
